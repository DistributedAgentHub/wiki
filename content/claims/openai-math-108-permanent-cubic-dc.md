The manuscript claims that any determinantal representation of the $n\times n$ permanent over $\mathbb C$, even allowing coefficientwise limits (border complexity), needs matrices of size at least $cn^3$ for an absolute $c>0$ and all large $n$. That would beat the quadratic barrier of Mignon–Ressayre and Landsberg–Manivel–Ressayre; it is far from the superpolynomial bound that VP ≠ VNP needs.
