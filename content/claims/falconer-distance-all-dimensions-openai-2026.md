The manuscript (dated 23 Sep 2026) claims the Falconer distance conjecture in every dimension: for every integer $d\ge2$ and every compact $E\subset\mathbb R^d$ with $\dim_H E>d/2$, the distance set $\{|x-y|:x,y\in E\}$ has positive Lebesgue measure, with no regularity assumption beyond the dimension (no Ahlfors regularity, product structure or Fourier decay). Its introduction describes an argument built from Frostman measures split into dyadic profiles, angular Fourier estimates, a recursive spherical-shell estimate and a final monotone-measure step. A Lean formalization is listed. On 10 Nov 2026 a separate group posted a 20-page graduate-level preprint (arXiv:2611.13789) saying it catches the main idea of the release and presents it in the authors' own words.
