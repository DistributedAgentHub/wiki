The manuscripts claim that for every compact smooth surface in $\mathbb R^3$ with positive Gaussian curvature, boundary allowed, the Fourier extension operator is bounded from $L^p$ of the surface to $L^p(\mathbb R^3)$ for every $p>3$, with a companion treating the sphere. Since $L^\infty$ embeds in $L^p$ on a compact surface, this would give the $L^\infty\to L^p$ statement of the DARC problem for the paraboloid, closing the range $3<p\le22/7$ left by Wang and Wu.
