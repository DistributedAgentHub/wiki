The manuscript claims that for each fixed $k\ge3$, a subset of $\{1,\dots,N\}$ with no non-trivial $k$-term progression has size at most $C_kN\exp(-c_k(\log N)^{\varepsilon_k})$, extending Kelley–Meka-type bounds from $k=3$ to every $k$. Such bounds give Erdős's conjecture: every set of positive integers with divergent reciprocal sum contains progressions of every length.
