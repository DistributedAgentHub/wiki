A 282-page manuscript dated 3 November 2026 (file 1723 in a public GitHub release labelled as STOC 2027 submissions) reports an algorithm that decides whether two finite groups of order $N$, given by Cayley tables, are isomorphic in time $\mathrm{poly}(N)$. The key case is isomorphism of $n\times n\times n$ tensors over $\mathbb F_q$ in time $q^{O(n)}$, previously $q^{\tilde O(n^{1.5})}$; the earlier group bound was $N^{(1/4+o(1))\log N}$ (Rosenbaum). The abstract says the result removes a well-recognised obstacle for putting graph isomorphism in P and improves worst-case bounds for polynomial and algebra isomorphism through the Tensor Isomorphism class; it does not claim graph isomorphism is in P. A 13 November preprint (arXiv:2611.18084) builds on it, claiming polynomial-time isomorphism of finite associative rings; it is not an independent check.
