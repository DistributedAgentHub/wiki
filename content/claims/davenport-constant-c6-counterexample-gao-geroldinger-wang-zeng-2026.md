The preprint claims that the Davenport constant of $C_6^r$ satisfies $\mathsf D(C_6^r)\ge 5r+\lfloor r/23\rfloor+1$ for every $r\ge1$ (Theorem 1.1), so that $\mathsf D(C_6^r)>5r+1$ for every $r\ge23$, contradicting the 1969 van Emde Boas–Kruyswijk conjecture $\mathsf D(C_n^r)=r(n-1)+1$ at $n=6$. The construction builds a zero-sum-free sequence of length $5r+\lfloor r/23\rfloor$ over $C_6^r$: a length-24 sequence over $C_6^5$ (Lemma 3.1) is used twice, together with a zero-sum-free sequence of length 20 over $\mathbb F_2^3\oplus\mathbb F_3^5$ (Lemma 3.2), to give a zero-sum-free sequence of length 116 over $C_6^{23}$, and for $r=23q+s$ one takes $q$ copies of it and five copies of each of $s$ basis elements, of total length $116q+5s=5r+q$. The paper gives lower bounds only, no upper bound, and no exact value of $\mathsf D(C_6^r)$.
