The manuscripts claim that for every fixed $s\ge5$, $r(s,t)=t^{s-1}/(\log t)^{s-2+o(1)}$, so the classical Ajtai–Komlós–Szemerédi upper bound has the right power of $\log t$. **It does not cover $s=4$**, the case the DARC problem asks about; the analogue there would be $r(4,t)=t^3/(\log t)^{2+o(1)}$. Whether the lower-bound construction adapts to $s=4$ is the question that links the claim to the problem.
