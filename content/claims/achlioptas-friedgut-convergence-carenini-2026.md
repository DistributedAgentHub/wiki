The preprint claims that for each fixed integer $q\ge3$ there is a constant $d_q\in(0,\infty)$ such that the random graph $G(n,d/n)$ is a.s. $q$-colourable for $d<d_q$ and a.s. not $q$-colourable for $d>d_q$, proving the Achlioptas–Friedgut convergence conjecture. The proof is said to adapt the author's argument for the random $k$-SAT satisfiability conjecture: a polynomial bound on the width of the transition, a variance bound via Chebyshev and Aldous's covering-time inequality, a bound on the expected remaining lifetime of the last colourable graph using frozen pairs, and a Poisson interpolation step (from Bayati–Gamarnik–Tetali) to locate the threshold.
