The manuscript claims an explicit finite set $F\subset\mathbb Z^3$ that tiles $\mathbb Z^3$ by translations but admits no tiling invariant under a finite-index subgroup, disproving the periodic tiling conjecture in dimension three. Thickening $F$ to unit cubes gives the same in $\mathbb R^3$, even with arbitrary real translations.
