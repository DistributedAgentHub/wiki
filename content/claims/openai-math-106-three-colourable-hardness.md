The manuscript claims that for every fixed $0<\delta<1/3$ a deterministic polynomial-time reduction from 3SAT produces simple graphs that are 3-colourable when the formula is satisfiable and have no independent set of $\delta n$ vertices otherwise. Then such graphs need more than $1/\delta$ colours, so colouring a 3-colourable graph with any fixed number $c\ge3$ of colours would be NP-hard.
