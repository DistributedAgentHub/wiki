The manuscript claims that for the Liouville function and any two fixed non-proportional affine forms, the ordinary correlation sum up to $X$ is $O(X/(\log X)^c)$ for an absolute $c>0$, and more generally a two-point Elliott-type cancellation for bounded multiplicative functions under a uniform non-pretentiousness condition. **It does not settle the DARC problem**, which asks for even order $k\ge4$ with logarithmic averaging; the claim is the order-2 case with the harder ordinary averaging.
