A manuscript in the Unique Games family claims a deterministic polynomial-time reduction from 3SAT to unweighted graphs in which satisfiable formulas give graphs with a vertex cover of about half the vertices and unsatisfiable ones force covers of almost all vertices. That would make approximating Vertex Cover within any constant factor $c<2$ NP-hard, which is far beyond the $\sqrt2$ needed for the DARC problem. The reduction is said to use established PCP and Label Cover results directly, not the family's claimed proof of the Unique Games Conjecture. It is a separate claim from `unique-games-theorem-openai`: if the UGC proof failed, this one could still stand, and the reverse.
