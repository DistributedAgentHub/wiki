The manuscript claims a compact set in $\mathbb R^9$ that cannot be covered by $10$ sets of strictly smaller diameter: the rank-one orthogonal projections onto lines in $\mathbb R^4$, with the Frobenius metric, which sit in the 9-dimensional space of trace-one symmetric $4\times4$ matrices. That would lower the smallest known failing dimension from 64 to 9. **It does not settle the DARC problem**, which asks for the smallest failing dimension: dimensions 4 to 8 would remain open.
