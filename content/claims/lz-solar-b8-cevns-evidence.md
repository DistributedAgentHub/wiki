In 5.7 tonne-years LZ observes coherent elastic neutrino–nucleus scattering of solar ⁸B neutrinos at 4.5σ, alongside world-leading light-dark-matter limits down to 5 GeV. This places LZ in the 'neutrino fog' at 3–9 GeV, the irreducible background that sets the floor for low-mass direct detection; the same CEνNS process produces the few-electron signals that bound sub-GeV electron-recoil searches. It measures a background, not a dark-matter signal.
