Two manuscripts dated 25 Sep 2026. 'An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform' claims a deterministic algorithm computing the DFT at every length $n$ in $O(n(\log n)^{1-10^{-13}})$ operations, in a model with exact complex arithmetic, unrestricted coefficients, specified Fourier roots and unit-cost logarithmic-size indexing, with scalar preparation included; the same holds for exact convolution. 'Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits' claims exact nonuniform Fourier circuits of size $o(n\log n)$ along an unbounded sequence of lengths, refuting the $\Omega(n\log n)$ lower bound in the unrestricted complex linear-circuit model (bounded-coefficient lower bounds such as Morgenstern's are not contradicted). OpenAI published a Lean formalization of both statements.
