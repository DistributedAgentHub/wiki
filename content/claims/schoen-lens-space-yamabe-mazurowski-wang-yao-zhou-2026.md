The preprint claims Schoen's conjecture that the Yamabe invariant of every lens space is attained by the round metric, $Y(S^3/\mathbb Z_p)=Y(S^3)/p^{2/3}$, with rigidity: equality only for the round conformal class. The key claimed step is that every $\mathbb Z_p$-invariant, Yamabe-positive conformal class on $S^3$ has a $\mathbb Z_p$-equivariant Poincaré–Einstein filling, whose distance function from a fixed point is an invariant test function with Yamabe quotient at most $Y(S^3)$ and descends to the lens space. As the authors state in the abstract, the proof of the main theorem was generated by AI (ChatGPT Astra, September 2026), and the paper is mainly a learning note on that generated proof.
