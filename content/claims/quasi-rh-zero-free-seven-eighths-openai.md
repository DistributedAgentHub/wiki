The manuscript 'The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis' (dated 30 Sep 2026, released 6 Oct) claims that $\zeta(s)$ and every Dirichlet $L$-function have no zeros with $\mathrm{Re}\, s > 7/8$, uniformly in the modulus, and the same for finite-order Hecke $L$-functions over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$; a companion manuscript claims a uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros, $1-\beta \ge c/\log q$, tracked as its own claim (`openai-math-003-landau-siegel-zeros`). A separate write-up gives an alternative proof of $\mathrm{Re}\, s > 11/12$. If correct, this would be the first zero-free half-plane of any width $<1$, but it leaves the strip $1/2 < \mathrm{Re}\, s \le 7/8$ open and does not change the zero-density supremum.
