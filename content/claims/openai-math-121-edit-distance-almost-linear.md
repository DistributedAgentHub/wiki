The manuscript claims, for every fixed rational $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$, a randomized algorithm that outputs a $(1+\varepsilon)$-approximation of the unit-cost edit distance of two strings of total length $N$ (integer symbols of polynomial size) in expected time $N^{1+o(1)}$, with success probability at least $2/3$. That is far below the $n^{2-\delta}$ the DARC problem asks for. The dependence of the $o(1)$ on $\varepsilon$ is not explicit, so the result is asymptotic, not a practical algorithm.
