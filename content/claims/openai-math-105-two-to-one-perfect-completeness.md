The manuscript claims that for every fixed rational $\delta\in(0,1)$ it is NP-hard to distinguish satisfiable 2-to-1 games from games of value at most $\delta$, on explicit unweighted instances whose alphabet depends only on $\delta$, with every label having exactly two preimages under each constraint. That is Khot's 2-to-1 conjecture with perfect completeness.
